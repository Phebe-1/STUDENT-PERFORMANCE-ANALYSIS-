# STUDENT-PERFORMANCE-ANALYSIS-DASHBOARD
A Power BI dashboard analyzing Student performance data to understand factor's that influence student's performance

**Project Overview**

​This project uses Power BI to analyse student performance data and explore patterns and relationships between these factors and Exam
Score. The analysis aims to understand what factors are associated with academic performance, identify students showing multiple indicators for further academic review, and provide data-driven insights that can help educators make informed decisions about academic support and student engagement.​

**Business Problem**

Educational institutions need to understand the factors associated with student academic performance in order to identify areas where students may benefit from additional support. 

**Objectives**

-Analyse study behaviour and academic performance​

-Examine the relationship between academic support and Exam Score​

-Analyse the impact of student motivation and lifestyle factors​

-Examine the influence of resources and learning environment​

-Analyse social and extracurricular factors​

-Identify students who may require additional academic support

**Tools used**

-Power BI = Data transformation, analysis and dashboard development

-Power Query = Data cleaning and preparation

-Kaggle = Dataset source

​**Insights**

Study behaviour and academic performance: Students with higher study hours recorded higher average Exam Scores and attendance compared with students with lower study hours, suggesting an association between stronger study engagement, attendance and academic performance

Academic support indicators: A Support Score combining six indicators was developed to identify students showing multiple indicators of lower academic engagement or performance. The score is intended to support further academic review rather than diagnose individual support needs.

**Business recommendations**

The School should prioritise students showing lower study engagement, attendance and academic performance for study-skills, revision and time-management support. 

Track Exam Scores, attendance, study engagement and Support Scores regularly to evaluate whether interventions are associated with improved student outcomes.
